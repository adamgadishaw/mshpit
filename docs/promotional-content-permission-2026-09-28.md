# Promotional content: permission and attribution

This release adds the owner's approved Terms scope: Mshpit social-media posts
and paid ads, with separate permission and credit on a visibly linked source
page. The same shared disclosure appears in the app and standalone website.
The Terms/Privacy acceptance version changes to `2026-09-28` for new signups;
no existing acceptance record or reuse setting is changed.

## What this does not enable

- No automatic marketing permission, opt-in migration, paid ad, media download,
  or promotional publishing job is created.
- Accepting Terms, uploading publicly, `landing_showcase`, artist-page reuse,
  email permission, analytics, and badges are not promotional-use consent.
- `/promotion-sources` is a public landing page, not a permission ledger. It has
  no campaign entries yet and cannot by itself supply an attribution link for
  an actual campaign. Catalogue `/photo-credits/` records are not member grants.
- There is no new Settings toggle or automatic withdrawal integration. Until
  a dedicated permission workflow is built, operations must manage explicit
  written permission and withdrawal through Support. Do not automate marketing
  selection from existing flags or assume an ad is paused by a database change.

## Before each campaign

1. Obtain item-specific written permission from the actual rights holder or
   authorized person through Support. Record the exact post/asset, permitted
   use, channels (including paid versus unpaid), start/end period, approved
   public credit and any editing limits. Keep correspondence private; do not
   put it, private member data or credentials in Git or on the source page.
2. Confirm the content is still public and approved immediately before use.
   Check for withdrawals, deletion and account/privacy changes. Obtain any
   additional music, performance or likeness rights needed. Do not run a minor's
   content without legal review and any required guardian authorization.
3. Prepare the actual campaign-specific credit entry with only agreed public
   information. Review it, then publish it with a stable unique anchor or URL
   before the promotion launches. Show the original public post where available
   and appropriate; do not infer uploader = photographer/copyright owner.
4. Visibly link directly to that entry from each ad/post/caption/description.
   If the format cannot carry the link, put credit with the promotion. A generic
   homepage or empty source landing page is insufficient.
5. Confirm any other applicable licence requires no stronger/direct attribution.
   This clause does not override catalogue licences or authorize unrelated ads.

## Withdrawal, expiry or deletion

Stop new uses when permission ends. Promptly pause/remove active ads and copies
under Mshpit's control, and remove/revise public credits where needed for privacy.
Do not treat caches, prior distribution, generic Terms-survival language, or a
private compliance record as permission to continue a campaign. Keep only the
private evidence needed for a documented, limited legal/operational purpose.
Third-party copies may not be retrievable; make reasonable removal requests.

Have qualified counsel review the wording and campaign process before relying
on it for a campaign, especially for minors, likenesses and live-music rights.
The policy does not claim blanket legal compliance or clear third-party rights.
