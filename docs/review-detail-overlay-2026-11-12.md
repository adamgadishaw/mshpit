# Review detail overlay — 12 November 2026

Base: `7be234d526bd177570f3948167b7ece782168bd8`.

## Finding and behavior

The base already had full review text and the author's media in `PostScreen`, but App replaced the originating screen. Feed review taps used `openShow`; an ordinary author review carrying the server's `archiveShowKey` could consequently be treated as a performance. The explicit show action also reused the author's review payload instead of resolving the canonical event aggregate.

Saved review taps now open a bounded, closable dialog while the originating screen remains mounted. X, Back, Escape and the backdrop dismiss it. Browser Back/Forward and `/post/:id` remain review destinations. The explicit **View event page** link resolves the canonical event separately, preserving the distinction between the author's score and the aggregate score. Feed review links now say **Open review**. Status, news, creator-update and attendance-ticket presentation keep their existing route behavior.

The review is read under the current account before full content is shown. Loading, retry and unavailable states do not reuse the navigation snapshot. Blocked/removed posts are suppressed, newer edits can reconcile by content version, and late linked-review/event responses cannot replace a later view or account. Gallery ordering and descriptors use the existing post media components. Text selection does not navigate.

The originating view's scroll, expanded sections and focus survive dismissal. The retained background is hidden from accessibility with `aria-hidden`; React Native 0.86 maps this to its native accessibility exclusion properties. Gallery Escape is handled once on keyup, including during its opening animation. Hidden gallery portals are unmounted while sign-in/report pages are active; their selected index is already preserved by navigation.
